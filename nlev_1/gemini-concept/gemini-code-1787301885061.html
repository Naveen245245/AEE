3-Phase & 1-Phase Induction Motors: Core Formulas, Torque Relations, Starters & Characteristics

1. Fundamental Speed & Slip Formulas

$$N_s = \frac{120 f}{P} \quad \text{(rpm)}$$
$$N_r = N_s(1 - s)$$
$$s = \frac{N_s - N_r}{N_s}$$
$$f_r = s \cdot f$$

2. Power Flow & Loss Relationships

Power Stage Sequence: Stator Input $\to$ Stator Cu/Iron Loss $\to$ Airgap Power ($P_{ag}$) $\to$ Rotor Cu Loss ($P_{rcu}$) $\to$ Mechanical Power ($P_m$) $\to$ Windage/Friction Loss $\to$ Shaft Output ($P_{sh}$).

$$P_{ag} : P_{rcu} : P_m = 1 : s : (1 - s)$$
  • Air-gap Power: $P_{ag} = 3 (I_2')^2 \frac{R_2'}{s} = \frac{P_{rcu}}{s}$
  • Rotor Copper Loss: $P_{rcu} = s \cdot P_{ag} = 3 (I_2')^2 R_2'$
  • Gross Mechanical Power: $P_m = (1-s) P_{ag} = \left(\frac{1-s}{s}\right) P_{rcu}$
  • Rotor Efficiency: $\eta_{\text{rotor}} = \frac{P_m}{P_{ag}} = 1 - s = \frac{N_r}{N_s}$
  • Electromagnetic Torque: $T_e = \frac{P_{ag}}{\omega_s} = \frac{P_m}{\omega_r}$

3. Torque Equations, Maximum Torque & Key Ratios

Electromagnetic Torque Equation

$$T = \frac{3}{\omega_s} \cdot \frac{V^2 \cdot \left(\frac{R_2}{s}\right)}{\left(R_1 + \frac{R_2}{s}\right)^2 + (X_1 + X_2)^2}$$
  • Low Slip Region ($s \ll 1$): $T \propto s$ (Linear characteristic).
  • High Slip Region ($s \approx 1$): $T \propto \frac{1}{s}$ (Hyperbolic).

Maximum (Breakdown) Torque ($T_{\max}$)

$$T_{\max} = \frac{3}{2\omega_s} \left[ \frac{V^2}{R_1 + \sqrt{R_1^2 + (X_1 + X_2)^2}} \right]$$
$$\text{Slip at } T_{\max}: s_m = \frac{R_2}{\sqrt{R_1^2 + (X_1 + X_2)^2}} \approx \frac{R_2}{X_2}$$
Key Insight: $T_{\max}$ is independent of Rotor Resistance ($R_2$), but the slip at maximum torque $s_m$ is directly proportional to $R_2$.

Corrected Torque Ratio Formulas

Ratio Type Exact / Standard Formula
Operating Torque to Max Torque $\left(\frac{T}{T_{\max}}\right)$ $$\frac{T}{T_{\max}} = \frac{2}{\frac{s}{s_m} + \frac{s_m}{s}} \quad \text{(Neglecting } R_1\text{)}$$
Starting Torque to Max Torque $\left(\frac{T_{st}}{T_{\max}}\right)$ $$\frac{T_{st}}{T_{\max}} = \frac{2 s_m}{1 + s_m^2}$$
Starting Torque to Full Load Torque $\left(\frac{T_{st}}{T_{fl}}\right)$ $$\frac{T_{st}}{T_{fl}} = \left( \frac{I_{st}}{I_{fl}} \right)^2 \cdot s_{fl}$$

5. Testing & Harmonic Phenomena

Equivalent Circuit Parameter Tests

  • No-Load Test (Open Rotor): Conducted at rated voltage, rated frequency. Gives magnetizing branch parameters ($R_0, X_0$) and constant losses.
  • Blocked Rotor Test: Conducted at reduced voltage ($5-15\%$), rated current. Gives equivalent leakage impedance ($R_e, X_e$) and copper losses.

Harmonic Effects (Cogging vs Crawling)

Phenomenon Cause Effect / Speed
Crawling 7th space harmonic torque Motor runs stably at low speed $N \approx \frac{N_s}{7}$.
Cogging (Magnetic Locking) Harmonic slot lock when $S_s = S_r$ Motor refuses to start. Prevented by skewing rotor slots.

6. Single-Phase Induction Motors (1-Phase IM)

Double Field Revolving Theory

  • Pulsating field = Two equal fields rotating in opposite directions at synchronous speed ($N_s$).
  • Forward Slip ($s_f$): $s_f = s$
  • Backward Slip ($s_b$): $s_b = 2 - s$
  • Net Torque: $T_{\text{net}} = T_f - T_b$ (At standstill $s=1 \implies T_f = T_b \implies T_{st} = 0$). Hence, 1-phase IM is NOT self-starting.

Types of 1-Phase IM & Applications

Type Starting Method / Angle ($\alpha$) Key Application
Split Phase Resistive auxiliary winding ($\alpha \approx 30^\circ$) Blowers, Centrifugal Pumps
Capacitor Start High $C$ in series with aux winding ($\alpha \approx 80^\circ$) Compressors, Refrigerators
Permanent Split (PSC) Run capacitor remains in circuit forever Ceiling Fans, Air Conditioners
Shaded Pole Shading coil (Cu band) creates weak rotating field Toy motors, Hair dryers
