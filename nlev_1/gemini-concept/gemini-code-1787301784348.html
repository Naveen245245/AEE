3-Phase & 1-Phase Induction Motors: Core Formulas, Torque Relations, Starters & Characteristics

1. Fundamental Speed & Slip Formulas

• Synchronous Speed: Ns = 120f / P (rpm)
• Rotor Speed: Nr = Ns(1 - s)
• Fractional Slip: s = (Ns - Nr) / Ns
• Rotor Frequency: fr = s · f

2. Power Flow & Loss Relationships

Power Stage Sequence: Stator Input $\to$ Stator Cu/Iron Loss $\to$ Airgap Power ($P_{ag}$) $\to$ Rotor Cu Loss ($P_{rcu}$) $\to$ Mechanical Power Developed ($P_m$) $\to$ Windage/Friction Loss $\to$ Shaft Output ($P_{sh}$).

Pag : Prcu : Pm = 1 : s : (1 - s)
  • Air-gap Power (Rotor Input): $P_{ag} = 3 I_2'^2 \frac{R_2'}{s} = \frac{P_{rcu}}{s}$
  • Rotor Copper Loss: $P_{rcu} = s \cdot P_{ag} = 3 I_2'^2 R_2'$
  • Gross Mechanical Power: $P_m = (1-s) P_{ag} = \frac{1-s}{s} P_{rcu}$
  • Rotor Efficiency: $\eta_{rotor} = \frac{P_m}{P_{ag}} = 1 - s = \frac{N_r}{N_s}$
  • Electromagnetic Torque: $T_e = \frac{P_{ag}}{\omega_s} = \frac{P_m}{\omega_r}$

3. Torque Equations, Maximum Torque & Ratios

Electromagnetic Torque Equation

T = \frac{3}{\omega_s} \cdot \frac{V^2 \cdot (R_2 / s)}{(R_1 + R_2 / s)^2 + (X_1 + X_2)^2}
  • Low Slip Region (Normal Operating Range): $T \propto s$ (Linear characteristic).
  • High Slip Region (Starting / Braking): $T \propto \frac{1}{s}$ (Rectangular hyperbola).

Maximum (Breakdown) Torque ($T_{max}$)

Tmax = \frac{3}{2\omega_s} \left[ \frac{V^2}{R_1 \pm \sqrt{R_1^2 + (X_1 + X_2)^2}} \right]
Slip at Tmax: sm = \frac{R_2}{\sqrt{R_1^2 + (X_1 + X_2)^2}} \approx \frac{R_2}{X_2}
Key Insight: Maximum Torque $T_{max}$ is independent of Rotor Resistance ($R_2$), but the slip at maximum torque $s_m$ is directly proportional to $R_2$.

Crucial Torque Ratio Formulas for Numerical Problems

Ratio Type Exact / Approximate Formula
Operating Torque to Max Torque ($\frac{T}{T_{max}}$) $\frac{T}{T_{max}} = \frac{2}{\frac{s}{s_m} + \frac{s_m}{s}}$   (Neglecting $R_1$)
Starting Torque to Max Torque ($\frac{T_{st}}{T_{max}}$) $\frac{T_{st}}{T_{max}} = \frac{2 s_m}{1 + s_m^2}$
Starting Torque to Full Load Torque ($\frac{T_{st}}{T_{fl}}$) $\frac{T_{st}}{T_{fl}} = \left( \frac{I_{st}}{I_{fl}} \right)^2 \cdot s_{fl}$

5. Testing & Harmonic Phenomena

Equivalent Circuit Parameter Tests

  • No-Load Test (Open Rotor): Conducted at rated voltage, rated frequency. Gives magnetizing branch parameters ($R_0, X_0$) and constant losses (Core + Friction/Windage).
  • Blocked Rotor Test: Conducted at reduced voltage ($5-15\%$), rated current. Gives equivalent leakage impedance ($R_e, X_e$) and full-load copper losses.

Harmonic Effects (Cogging vs Crawling)

Phenomenon Cause Effect / Speed
Crawling 7th space harmonic torque Motor runs stably at very low speed $N \approx \frac{N_s}{7}$.
Cogging (Magnetic Locking) Harmonic slot lock when $S_s = S_r$ or $S_s - S_r = 3p$ Motor refuses to start. Prevented by skewing rotor slots or using prime slot ratios.

6. Single-Phase Induction Motors (1-Phase IM)

Double Field Revolving Theory

  • Single-phase pulsating field = Two equal fields rotating in opposite directions at synchronous speed ($N_s$).
  • Forward Slip ($s_f$): $s_f = s$
  • Backward Slip ($s_b$): $s_b = 2 - s$
  • Net Torque: $T_{net} = T_f - T_b$ (At standstill $s=1 \implies T_f = T_b \implies T_{st} = 0$). Hence, 1-phase IM is NOT self-starting.

Types of 1-Phase IM & Applications

Type Starting Method / Angle ($\alpha$) Key Application
Split Phase Resistive auxiliary winding ($\alpha \approx 30^\circ$) Blowers, Centrifugal Pumps
Capacitor Start High $C$ in series with aux winding ($\alpha \approx 80^\circ$) Compressors, Refrigerators
Permanent Split (PSC) Run capacitor remains in circuit forever Ceiling Fans, Air Conditioners
Shaded Pole Shading coil (Cu band) creates weak rotating field Toy motors, Hair dryers (Very low $T_{st}$)
