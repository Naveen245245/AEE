## PART A — QUANTITATIVE APTITUDE (shortcuts, not teaching)

1. **Fraction → % table (memorize):** 1/2=50, 1/3=33.3, 1/4=25, 1/5=20, 1/6=16.67, 1/7≈14.3, 1/8=12.5, 1/9≈11.1, 1/10=10, 1/12≈8.33. Speeds up %/profit problems 5×.
2. **Speed–time–distance:** average speed for two equal distances `= 2ab/(a+b)`. Trains: crossing a platform adds platform length; relative speed adds (opposite) / subtracts (same direction).
3. **Boats/streams:** downstream `= u+v`, upstream `= u−v`; boat speed `u = (down+up)/2`, stream `v = (down−up)/2`. Trap: swapping "still-water" and "stream" speeds.
4. Work & time: rate `= 1/days`; combine by adding rates; pipes & cisterns same logic (outlet = negative).
5. SI: `A = P(1+rt)`. **CI: `A = P(1 + r/100)^t`**; CI − SI over 2 years `= P(r/100)²` (approximation exam-favourite). Trap: SI vs CI difference at n=2.
6. Profit/loss: SP = CP(1±p/100); **discount is on Marked Price** (not CP). Successive discounts: `a+b − ab/100`.
7. Ratio & partnership: profit shared ∝ (capital × time). Alligation: cheaper/dearer mixing cross-rule.
8. **Number series / missing term:** test arithmetic (+d), geometric (×r), alternating two-series, squares/cubes, primes, Fibonacci. PYQ 2023 Q122/2025 Q113 type.
9. Averages: new average after replacement; weighted average.
10. Trig (sparingly): sin30=1/2, cos60=1/2, tan45=1. Mensuration: area/perimeter of circle, rectangle, triangle, cylinder volume.

## PART B — REASONING (each has a recipe — drill the recipe)

11. **Coding-decoding (PYQ 2025):** letter-shift codes, reverse alphabet (A=Z), word-number codes, symbol-codes (P ÷ Q = "P is son of Q" family trees — PYQ 2025 Q123). Recipe: decode the operator table first, then walk the tree top-down.
12. **Statements & conclusions / syllogism (PYQ 2025 Q132):** always test the *extreme* case; two statements → only one valid venn arrangement. Watch "all/some/no" quantifier + "some not".
13. **Letter/number/analogy series:** map position (A=1…Z=26), then detect the step (e.g. +2,+3 alternate; prime gaps; reverse).
14. **Direction sense:** net displacement via Pythagoras; right/left turn at 90°; draw it. Clock: hands coincide ~every 65⅝ min.
15. **Seating / circular / linear puzzles + ordering (height/age/rank):** anchor on the strongest fixed statement, then fill. Blood relation: build the family tree, label M/F.
16. Missing-number puzzles (matrix/row) — find the row rule then apply to the blank. Odd-one-out: classify by property (vowel count, prime, colour…).

## PART C — ENGLISH (grammar + vocab)

17. **One-word substitution (PYQ 2025 Q101):** test the "one who…/a place where…/a study of…" patterns; wrong options = near-synonyms. 
18. **Antonyms/synonyms (PYQ 2023 Q148/2025):** pick by elimination; if stuck, choose the word of opposite *tone* to the stem.
19. **Sentence improvement / error-spotting (PYQ 2025 Q126 quoted-part style):** check subject–verb agreement, tense sequence, preposition after verb, parallelism; the "improved" part usually fixes only ONE error.
20. **Sentence rearrangement (P Q R S, PYQ 2023/2025):** find the opener (introduces subject, no pronoun referencing earlier), then the logical flow (time/cause→effect); pronouns (it/this/they) must follow their noun.
21. Fill in the blank: prepositions & conjunctions (`between/among`, `since/for`, `although/despite`), articles, determiners. Grammar core: present/past/future + perfect/continuous; active↔passive and direct↔indirect only swap voice/reporting — never the tense logic.
