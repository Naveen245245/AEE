## Question No. 1
**Answer: A ((3 - 18x)e⁻⁶ˣ)**

### Explanation:
The given differential equation is:
d²y/dx² + 12 dy/dx + 36y = 0

The characteristic equation is:
r² + 12r + 36 = 0
(r + 6)² = 0
r = -6, -6 (repeated roots)

The general solution is:
y(x) = (C₁ + C₂x)e⁻⁶ˣ

Applying initial conditions:
y(0) = C₁ = 3
dy/dx = C₂e⁻⁶ˣ - 6(C₁ + C₂x)e⁻⁶ˣ
At x = 0: dy/dx = C₂ - 6C₁ = -36
C₂ - 18 = -36
C₂ = -18

Particular solution: y = (3 - 18x)e⁻⁶ˣ

## Question No. 2
**Answer: D (A point charge)**

### Explanation:
Gauss's law states that the total electric flux through any closed surface is proportional to the total charge enclosed by that surface. While Gauss's law is always true, it is most useful for determining the electric field when the charge distribution has high symmetry (spherical, cylindrical, planar). A point charge has spherical symmetry, making it ideal for application of Gauss's law. Non-uniform charge distributions, finite sheets, and finite lines lack the symmetry required for simple application of Gauss's law to find the field.

## Question No. 4
**Answer: A (Shunt reactors)**

### Explanation:
The Ferranti effect is a phenomenon where the receiving-end voltage of a long transmission line becomes higher than the sending-end voltage, especially under light load or no-load conditions. This happens due to the charging current of the line capacitance. Shunt reactors are inductive devices connected in parallel (shunt) with the line. They absorb the reactive power generated by the line capacitance, thereby controlling voltage rise and mitigating the Ferranti effect.

## Question No. 5
**Answer: C (Lead compensator)**

### Explanation:
A lead compensator introduces a phase lead, which improves the transient response (speed of response and damping) of a system. However, it generally provides only a limited improvement in the steady-state response (accuracy) compared to a lag compensator. Lag compensators primarily improve steady-state accuracy but can slow down the transient response.

x_{k+1} = x_k - (x_k³ - 5x_k + 7) / (3x_k² - 5)
x_{k+1} = (3x_k³ - 5x_k - x_k³ + 5x_k - 7) / (3x_k² - 5)
